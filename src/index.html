case-study/01

Real-time LLM-News Trading Bot

Problem

Market-moving news arrives from many sources at once, much of it duplicated. Feeding all of it to an LLM is slow and expensive, and live exchange data isn't always available.

Approach

  • Event-driven, dual-pass agent ingesting 50+ NIFTY symbols and 4 news sources.
  • Hash-based dedup buffer drops repeated stories before they reach the model.
  • News-First LLM pipeline emits structured-JSON trade signals.
  • Real-time floating-P&L audits on dual 15/30-minute cycles.
  • Falls back to sentiment-only reasoning when NSE data is restricted.

Tech stack

PythonFastAPISQLAlchemyAPSchedulerGemini 2.5

Impact

−60% Gemini token usage from deduplication, with signals that keep flowing even when market data doesn't.

SMIRP — Social Media Interactive Resource Planner for Students

Approach

case-study/03 · ViTECoN, IEEE · May 2023

Credit Scoring using Swarm Intelligence for Feature Selection and PSO-Trained ANN Classification

Problem

Credit datasets carry many noisy, correlated features, and gradient-trained networks can settle in poor local minima.

Approach

  • Swarm-intelligence search selects a compact, informative feature subset.
  • Particle Swarm Optimization trains the neural network's weights in place of backpropagation alone.
  • The resulting ANN classifies applicants by credit risk.

Tech stack

Particle Swarm OptimizationFeature selectionNeural networks
